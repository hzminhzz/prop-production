# ADR: Shared five-minute source signals, Guardian financial independence

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED TARGET ARCHITECTURE (Q1–Q16; Q2=B, Q13=B, all others A). Documentation-only, not implemented, deployed, scientifically qualified or trading-authorized.
**Companion:** `copytrade-pm/docs/decisions/2026-11-11-canonical-5m-checkpoint-grill.md`.
**Current installed contract:** `V4ShadowProjection` uses original transition T0+300 and remains unchanged.

## Approved boundary

The planned Canonical Wallet Signal Authority (CWSA) in `copytrade-pm` owns one verified selected-account position checkpoint every five UTC minutes and one versioned source target-state signal identity. It compares complete, provenance-backed economic position states; a changed sampled target may be published once with original checkpoint/cutoff and proof. It does **not** own the follower account or authorize orders.

The existing Guardian is the sole **protected financial authority**. The proposed Proof-Carrying Execution Lane is a deeper lifecycle **inside Guardian**, not a new service or risk engine. It validates independently authentic source receipts and native market/account evidence, verifies the active signed policy and frozen k, applies account risk and HALT, durably commits intents before dispatch, and reconciles broker orders, fills and uncertain outcomes. It may verify a checkpoint and freshness but may **not** manufacture a second leader clock or alternative source signal identity.

Paper PM03/PM04 remains independent simulated finance. Each consumer owns its source cursor and financial effect identity, not source truth. Shared signal data is not a broker order, a Guardian permission or a global financial transaction. Signed qualification, owner authorization and account-specific native permission remain separate admission gates. Read-only OBSERVE must not require LIVE financial qualification.

This new policy differs economically from existing first-transition T0+300: source changes near a five-minute boundary may be acted upon with substantially less than 300 seconds of age. Intra-window round trips may be invisible. Do **not** promote existing prospective/backtest T+300 evidence as evidence for this policy. Do not weaken unknown-outcome recovery, broker reconciliation, protected HALT, journal nonrewind, or financial conservation.

## Additional owner-approved policy decisions and Guardian implications

- **Q3=A:** Each UTC five-minute source checkpoint is evaluated using a fresh, complete, verified observation obtained within a bounded window; retain separate checkpoint, provider-state, local-receipt and validation/publish timestamps. The maximum age and grace limits are not yet approved. Never transform a late/replayed snapshot into an earlier known time.
- **Q4=A:** A CWSA signal is the complete desired source-account position vector, not an executable order delta. Guardian independently verifies the source receipt, frozen signed k, native source/follower valuations, its own account holdings, unresolved broker effects and risk. Repeated target receipt cannot bypass idempotent intent/execution reconciliation.
- **Q5=A:** Source-driven increases, reductions, closes and reversals all follow the same five-minute checkpoints. Account loss limits, HALT, incident-driven protective risk reduction and unknown-outcome recovery continue to operate independently between checkpoints. Delaying source closes changes economics and requires separate prospective and costed qualification; no automatic trading permit.

**Q2=B was subsequently approved after the Vike review.** Vike REST/MCP supports wallet-filtered 5-minute position history and an approximately five-minute latest all-wallet positions archive. However, source-state `state_ts` can precede ingestion `snapshot_ts` by 5–10 minutes; snapshot writes are irregular, files appear later, older stale rows can represent recently closed positions and native quantity fields use Float64. Do not label a provider position row an account-complete, fresh or exact signed checkpoint without independent proof. Official per-checkpoint Hyperliquid REST may bypass the existing user-WS subscription budget but requires separate all-DEX completeness and rate/cutoff verification. See the companion CWSA ADR for evidence and alternatives.

## Owner-approved Round 3 decisions (2026-11-12)

- **Q2=B — authoritative source:** Only CWSA's qualified complete native Hyperliquid per-account/all-selected-DEX **REST** checkpoints establish the leader target for this new policy. Vike may assist raw-event collection, research and independent comparison; delayed, incomplete or Float64 vendor position rows do not independently qualify. A signed source receipt must carry independently verifiable original native evidence. A verified native REST feed at 14-account/selected-DEX scale remains an unproven operational target.
- **Q6=A — recovery baseline:** Missing or invalid scheduled source evidence blocks new opening exposure. Restoration establishes an independently verified **nonactionable** baseline; protected Guardian may independently authorize only evidence-backed risk reduction toward that complete recovered target, never an opening or opposite-direction flip. A subsequent valid continuous economic change is required for new source-driven exposure. No missing observation implies an account close, no source rebaseline clears broker UNKNOWN or account HALT.
- **Q7=A — distinct IDs:** Every source-account/scope/policy epoch/checkpoint obtains a durable outcome even when no new signal exists. CWSA emits an actionable target-change signal only for verified changes between consecutive complete valid checkpoints. Receipt identity, original evidence SHA, economic hash, target-change ID and Guardian consumer effect ID are distinct. Replays/unchanged checks do not generate fresh intents; protective reduction-only recovery has an explicit, separately verifiable disposition.
- **Q8=A — no automatic target chasing:** One changed eligible checkpoint creates at most one **guarded new-risk attempt per protected financial consumer**; unchanged leader targets never top up or retry prior exposure. Guardian persists account/pod-specific decision, reservation and intent before any broker dispatch and reconciles uncertain external outcomes. A partial fill or unknown acknowledgment does not license another order. Source gap, late packet and reconnect are not means to create a second attempt. Exact pre-dispatch expiry and reduction/reversal sequencing remain dependent design choices.

**Existing implementation mismatch:** The checked `prop_production.v4_timing.V4ShadowProjection` creates its own `INTENT_TIME_MS=300_000` and stores `pending_since_ms`, so it is **not** an implementation of the new CWSA checkpoint policy. Current `Guardian.reserve`/`dispatch` enforce a synthetic-only execution gate. Neither component may be described as live-ready under these ADRs. The financial owner and broker-unknown reconciliation invariants remain in force.

## Unresolved contracts for next grill

**Settled Q1–Q16:** native REST complete grid checkpoints with measured bounds, signed full target vector, source recovery/activation baselines, one-shot new-risk with pre-dispatch-only re-evaluation, pod-independent risk and safe broker netting, distinct copy mandates, full-target later increases, and pause without implicit flatten. **Unsettled:** measured numerical freshness/grace and cross-DEX skew, source native REST capacity; complete signed wire/protocol versions and trust anchor; finite opportunity expiry, partial fills, reversal and unknown broker disposition; per-mandate policy epoch revisions; independent prospective economic evidence and signed reversible cutover.

## HyperX Agent API audit — no protected-authority change (2026-11-12)

The [HyperX Copy-Trading API catalog](https://api-service.hyperx.trade/api/catalog/copy-trading) documents a potentially useful one-request `POST /api/hyperliquid/allDexsClearinghouseState` that returns per-DEX positions for an arbitrary wallet, and a merged `POST /api/hyperliquid/clearinghouseState`. Unlike its [Data API catalog](https://data-api.hyperx.trade/api-catalog), this is authenticated with a **Trading API token**, capable of changing follow configurations and submitting/canceling orders. It is not a least-privilege read-only source credential. Do **not** introduce this token into CWSA, PM03/04 or a Guardian source receiver.

HyperX's proprietary copy engine supports follow increases/decreases, immediate copying of existing positions, vendor-defined proportional/fixed-size follow modes and manual order actions. None of these constitutes a shared canonical five-minute target-state signal or a Guardian-committed, account-bound single-use financial intent. It does not replace our protected Propr/Bybit broker proof, unknown-order recovery, frozen-k sizing or financial HALT. It may be evaluated only as a nonauthoritative isolated **read-only comparison** with a safe credential boundary, independently checked source timestamps, exact DEX census and native provenance. Its live service and provider reliability have not been qualified. Source authority decision Q2=B (native Hyperliquid REST) is **unchanged**.

## HyperX copy-relationship architecture comparison (research-only, no decision) — 2026-11-12

The public [HyperX Copy-Trading API catalog](https://api-service.hyperx.trade/api/catalog/copy-trading) exposes a first-class relationship from `copy_wallet_address` (leader) to `wallet_address` (follower), plus sizing/risk switches, `copy_current_position` (bootstrap), `copy_increase_as_open_position` (late or missed initial open), and separate `follow_increase`/`follow_decrease` actions. Copy-config delete is documented as **soft-delete**, preserving open follower positions. Public error codes distinguish insufficient balance, minimum-size rejection, missing master/follower position, provider source/follower state errors, rate-limit and order errors; documented retry advice does not establish safe handling of an **uncertain dispatched order**.

These are **product API contracts**, not verified HyperX internal engine algorithms. Our existing signed `portfolio_release_v1.members[]` is already a copy-mandate concept; it binds selected source, account scope, `k` and allowed markets to protected pods. Do not add a third service. Retain one CWSA source signal, independent protected account holdings and Guardian-authorized broker intent. New design questions: activation baseline versus copy-current; whether a later leader increase can open a follower position that missed initial entry; pause/retire versus explicit flatten; safe re-evaluation of pre-dispatch rejection without resending uncertain orders. These were **unapproved** at the time of the comparative audit and have since been explicitly approved as Q13=B and Q14–Q16=A below. The approval is a target decision only.

## Owner-approved Round 4 — Guardian and copy-mandate boundary (2026-11-12)

- **Q9=A — measured bounded freshness:** Guardian verifies the source checkpoint's separate native state, acquisition, local-receipt and publication clocks under a signed policy. Cross-DEX skew and observation-age limits must be established by measured native REST load/latency evidence before cutover, not guessed from the five-minute schedule. Local account/quote recency remains separately verified.
- **Q10=A — selected-DEX completeness:** Every required selected wallet/DEX requires explicit native return, including an authenticated empty array for a legitimate zero position. A missing DEX is **not** zero, and incomplete new-risk targets cannot be admitted.
- **Q11=A — financial ownership then net order:** Each pod's frozen target, existing attributed positions, loss budget, limits and broker effects remain independent. Guardian may aggregate only simultaneously eligible signed pod requests into one guarded net order per destination account/instrument/checkpoint, when native broker capabilities permit. Loss, fee, partial-fill and virtual attribution reconcile to one actual broker effect without fictitious cash, cross-pod double counts or unapproved positions. Pending/unknown effects block conflicting openings.
- **Q12=A — source proof and signed copy mandate:** CWSA owns source checkpoint identity/target. Guardian independently verifies a signed checkpoint proof bound to original native evidence and separate account-bound, frozen copy mandate/release, plus native quotes, follower equity/positions, reconciled orders and protected risk. A source signature is not broker authorization. Introduce a prospective wire/release schema later; preserve existing `source_book_v1` and `portfolio_release_v1` meanings.
- **Q13=B — retry only before possible broker side effect:** A temporarily blocked *pre-dispatch* opportunity may be re-evaluated within its original evidence/deadline window, with newly verified quotes, account and risk, **only when no broker dispatch could have occurred**. It cannot bypass protected HALT or a terminal risk/policy denial. Once an order is committed for dispatch or its outcome is uncertain, never place a blind replacement; keep broker-order reconciliation authoritative.
- **Q14=A — activation as baseline:** A newly activated or materially revised signed leader-to-pod mandate does not copy already-open source positions. First verified complete book is nonactionable for new exposure. Activation does not inherit or erase historical source/financial journal state.
- **Q15=A — missed initial entry then genuine increase:** If a new source change genuinely increases leader exposure at a later eligible checkpoint, a previously unfilled follower may make **one fresh guarded attempt toward the complete new frozen-k target** instead of only the incremental leader difference; this cannot duplicate or override uncertain older broker orders. The resulting later entry changes follower economics and must be evaluated.
- **Q16=A — pause/retire is not flatten:** Pause disables all new-risk openings for the signed mandate but retains existing follower inventory, attribution, durable HALTs and unresolved financial effects under Guardian protection. Reducing/closing risk remains available under independent account limits. Flattening is a separate explicit protected action, not a side effect of disable, delete or source GAP.

**Existing implementation gap:** Current `ReleaseVerifier` strictly requires `portfolio_release_v1` and a fixed `batch_seconds=300` and knows no copy-mandate activation/paused/retired state. Existing `V4ShadowProjection` owns a separate T0+300 eligibility clock. Both are old-policy implementation facts, **not** evidence Q9–Q16 is deployed. Next design frontier: new signed mandate/source schema and epoch cutover, finite pre-dispatch expiry and unknown/reversal semantics, isolation and qualification gates. No new microservice or source scheduler is authorized.

## Migration constraints

The currently installed V4 timing projection, Guardian journal, Planner/receiver, Execution Session diagnostic, protected services, broker routes and financial authorization remain unchanged. Future transition requires a **new signed policy version and prospective epoch**, independent compatibility review, matching 16-paper/Guardian source IDs, realistic follower economics and separate authorization. No historical receipt interpretation may change through this ADR.
