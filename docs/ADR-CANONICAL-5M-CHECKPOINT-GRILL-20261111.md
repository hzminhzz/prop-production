# ADR: Shared five-minute source signals, Guardian financial independence

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED TARGET ARCHITECTURE (Q1=A; Q2=B; Q3=A; Q4=A; Q5=A; Q6=A; Q7=A; Q8=A). Documentation-only, not implemented, deployed, scientifically qualified or trading-authorized.
**Companion:** `copytrade-pm/docs/decisions/2026-11-11-canonical-5m-checkpoint-grill.md`.
**Current installed contract:** `V4ShadowProjection` uses original transition T0+300 and remains unchanged.

## Approved boundary

The planned Canonical Wallet Signal Authority (CWSA) in `copytrade-pm` owns one verified selected-account position checkpoint every five UTC minutes and one versioned source target-state signal identity. It compares complete, provenance-backed economic position states; a changed sampled target may be published once with original checkpoint/cutoff and proof. It does **not** own the follower account or authorize orders.

The existing Guardian is the sole **protected financial authority**. The proposed Proof-Carrying Execution Lane is a deeper lifecycle **inside Guardian**, not a new service or risk engine. It validates independently authentic source receipts and native market/account evidence, verifies the active signed policy and frozen k, applies account risk and HALT, durably commits intents before dispatch, and reconciles broker orders, fills and uncertain outcomes. It may verify a checkpoint and freshness but may **not** manufacture a second leader clock or alternative source signal identity.

Paper PM03/PM04 remains independent simulated finance. Each consumer owns its source cursor and financial effect identity, not source truth. Shared signal data is not a broker order, a Guardian permission or a global financial transaction. Signed qualification, owner authorization and account-specific native permission remain separate admission gates. Read-only OBSERVE must not require LIVE financial qualification.

This new policy differs economically from existing first-transition T0+300: source changes near a five-minute boundary may be acted upon with substantially less than 300 seconds of age. Intra-window round trips may be invisible. Do **not** promote existing prospective/backtest T+300 evidence as evidence for this policy. Do not weaken unknown-outcome recovery, broker reconciliation, protected HALT, journal nonrewind, or financial conservation.

## Additional owner-approved policy decisions and Guardian implications

- **Q3=A:** Each UTC five-minute source checkpoint is evaluated using a fresh, complete, verified observation obtained within a bounded window; retain separate checkpoint, provider-state, local-receipt and validation/publish timestamps. The maximum age and grace limits are not yet approved. Never transform a late/replayed snapshot into an earlier known time.
- **Q4=A:** A CWSA signal is the complete desired source-account position vector, not an executable order delta. Guardian independently verifies the source receipt, frozen signed k, native source/follower valuations, its own account holdings, unresolved broker effects and risk. Repeated target receipt cannot bypass idempotent intent/execution reconciliation.
- **Q5=A:** Source-driven increases, reductions, closes and reversals all follow the same five-minute checkpoints. Account loss limits, HALT, incident-driven protective risk reduction and unknown-outcome recovery continue to operate independently between checkpoints. Delaying source closes changes economics and requires separate prospective and costed qualification; no automatic trading permit.

**Q2=B was subsequently approved after the Vike review.** Vike REST/MCP supports wallet-filtered 5-minute position history and an approximately five-minute latest all-wallet positions archive. However, source-state `state_ts` can precede ingestion `snapshot_ts` by 5–10 minutes; snapshot writes are irregular, files appear later, older stale rows can represent recently closed positions and native quantity fields use Float64. Do not label a provider position row an account-complete, fresh or exact signed checkpoint without independent proof. Official per-checkpoint Hyperliquid REST may bypass the existing user-WS subscription budget but requires separate all-DEX completeness and rate/cutoff verification. See the companion CWSA ADR for evidence and alternatives.

## Owner-approved Round 3 decisions (2026-11-12)

- **Q2=B — authoritative source:** Only CWSA's qualified complete native Hyperliquid per-account/all-selected-DEX **REST** checkpoints establish the leader target for this new policy. Vike may assist raw-event collection, research and independent comparison; delayed, incomplete or Float64 vendor position rows do not independently qualify. A signed source receipt must carry independently verifiable original native evidence. A verified native REST feed at 14-account/selected-DEX scale remains an unproven operational target.
- **Q6=A — recovery baseline:** Missing or invalid scheduled source evidence blocks new opening exposure. Restoration establishes an independently verified **nonactionable** baseline; protected Guardian may independently authorize only evidence-backed risk reduction toward that complete recovered target, never an opening or opposite-direction flip. A subsequent valid continuous economic change is required for new source-driven exposure. No missing observation implies an account close, no source rebaseline clears broker UNKNOWN or account HALT.
- **Q7=A — distinct IDs:** Every source-account/scope/policy epoch/checkpoint obtains a durable outcome even when no new signal exists. CWSA emits an actionable target-change signal only for verified changes between consecutive complete valid checkpoints. Receipt identity, original evidence SHA, economic hash, target-change ID and Guardian consumer effect ID are distinct. Replays/unchanged checks do not generate fresh intents; protective reduction-only recovery has an explicit, separately verifiable disposition.
- **Q8=A — no automatic target chasing:** One changed eligible checkpoint creates at most one **guarded new-risk attempt per protected financial consumer**; unchanged leader targets never top up or retry prior exposure. Guardian persists account/pod-specific decision, reservation and intent before any broker dispatch and reconciles uncertain external outcomes. A partial fill or unknown acknowledgment does not license another order. Source gap, late packet and reconnect are not means to create a second attempt. Exact pre-dispatch expiry and reduction/reversal sequencing remain dependent design choices.

**Existing implementation mismatch:** The checked `prop_production.v4_timing.V4ShadowProjection` creates its own `INTENT_TIME_MS=300_000` and stores `pending_since_ms`, so it is **not** an implementation of the new CWSA checkpoint policy. Current `Guardian.reserve`/`dispatch` enforce a synthetic-only execution gate. Neither component may be described as live-ready under these ADRs. The financial owner and broker-unknown reconciliation invariants remain in force.

## Unresolved contracts for next grill

**Previously settled (Q3–Q5):** fresh bounded source observation rather than falsely exact as-of sampling (Q3), full leader target-state signal (Q4), and five-minute-grid **source** reductions/entries with independent immediate financial protection (Q5). **Unsettled:** numeric freshness/grace and evidence lag; missing/stale/incomplete sample recovery details; signal/source-epoch identities; pre-dispatch rejection and opportunity expiry; reduction/reversal and partial-fill disposition; cross-consumer proof version; native REST provider-rate/coherence verification (Q2 target chosen); exactly-once local financial intent versus uncertain remote outcome; signed release and reversible activation policy.

## Migration constraints

The currently installed V4 timing projection, Guardian journal, Planner/receiver, Execution Session diagnostic, protected services, broker routes and financial authorization remain unchanged. Future transition requires a **new signed policy version and prospective epoch**, independent compatibility review, matching 16-paper/Guardian source IDs, realistic follower economics and separate authorization. No historical receipt interpretation may change through this ADR.
